## 2025-05-14 - [Copy-to-Clipboard with Feedback]
**Learning:** In interactive sections hidden behind toggles (like "Donate" or "Details"), adding a copy-to-clipboard button with immediate visual feedback (icon swap + toast) significantly reduces friction for users who need to take action on that data.
**Action:** Always include a success state (like a checkmark icon) and a toast notification when implementing clipboard actions to confirm the "invisible" background process was successful.
